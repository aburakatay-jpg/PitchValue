# Phase 4 physical-device checklist

This checklist prepares acceptance work; it does not claim that a physical iPhone run has happened.

## First launch

- Confirm the native launch surface remains `#08111F` with no white flash.
- Verify the 18+ confirmation appears before tutorial content on a clean install.
- Verify Exit leaves PitchValue locked and does not route into Today.
- Complete all three tutorial pages and confirm the final action reaches Today as Guest.
- Relaunch and confirm completed first launch does not flash the age gate or tutorial.

## Layout and input

- Test a small iPhone, a standard iPhone, and a Pro Max-sized device.
- Test notch, Dynamic Island, and home-indicator safe areas.
- Test the largest practical Dynamic Type sizes without clipped legal text or unreachable actions.
- Test Email and Password focus, keyboard avoidance, scrolling, secure entry, and return keys.
- Confirm long localized plan, legal, and Profile text wraps without horizontal clipping.

## Accessibility and visual safety

- Run VoiceOver through age confirmation, tutorial, Guest entry, Profile, auth form, and paywall.
- Confirm page position, disabled account/store actions, errors, and entitlement state are announced.
- Confirm dark OLED contrast and that status meaning is never conveyed by color alone.
- Confirm no mock identity, price, purchase success, trial eligibility, or restoration result appears.

## Existing public surfaces

- Recheck Today, Explore, Match Detail, All Markets, and Final Check navigation and retained-data behavior.
- Confirm Guest can browse public surfaces without account creation.
- Confirm no shadow analysis is visible.
